Accessibility Statement

thebutlr.com (marketing website)

Last reviewed: 17 October 2026

Current status This is a self-assessment by our own team, not an independent accredited audit. We believe this site is largely usable with a keyboard and screen reader and meets most of WCAG 2.2 AA, but we know of specific gaps, listed below, that we're still working through.

1.Our commitment

We want this website to be usable by as many people as possible, including people who use a keyboard only, a screen reader, voice control, or who need larger text, higher contrast, or reduced motion. We're targeting WCAG 2.2 at level AA as our standard.

2.What we've checked and fixed

  • A visible "Skip to content" link for keyboard users, and a proper <main> landmark around the page's core content.
  • A visible focus style on every interactive element, including form fields (previously the default outline was removed without a visible replacement).
  • The eligibility quiz is operable by keyboard alone, keeps focus trapped inside it while open, and returns focus to the control that opened it when closed.
  • Decorative icons are hidden from screen readers (aria-hidden) so they don't add noise to the page's reading order.
  • Respect for prefers-reduced-motion across the page's animations.
  • Form fields use real, associated labels (not placeholder-only text), mark required fields in visible text, and use standard autocomplete attributes.

3.Known issues we're still working on

  • We've checked contrast on the main text and UI colour pairs used across the page, but have not yet completed an exhaustive, pixel-by-pixel check of every text-over-image or text-over-gradient area at every screen size.
  • The eligibility quiz has been tested for keyboard operability, but not yet end-to-end with a screen reader; step changes inside it are not currently announced automatically to assistive technology (no live region).
  • We have not yet completed a formal check of 200% browser zoom and 320px-wide reflow across every section of the page.
  • This statement, and the fixes behind it, have not yet been reviewed by an accredited third-party accessibility auditor.

4.Telling us about a problem

If you find something on this site you can't access, or have feedback on its accessibility, please email us and we'll aim to respond within 5 working days.

5.Enforcement

If you're not happy with our response, you can contact the Equality Advisory and Support Service (EASS), or, for a UK GDPR / data-protection matter, the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).
