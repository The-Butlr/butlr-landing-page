4.How we block non-essential cookies until you consent

Analytics scripts on this site are not loaded as ordinary scripts. They sit inertly on the page (marked in a way browsers won't execute or fetch) until you choose "Accept all" or switch Analytics on in "Manage preferences" — only then does the browser request and run them. Choosing "Reject non-essential" keeps them switched off.

5.Withdrawing consent

You can withdraw or change your consent at any time using the "Cookie settings" link in the footer of this site, or the button in Section 2 above. Withdrawing is exactly as easy as giving consent in the first place.

6.Browser controls

Most browsers also let you block or delete cookies and local storage directly in their settings. Doing so may affect how this site remembers your preferences, but will not affect the Platform, which is governed separately by our Privacy Policy.

7.Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the cookies or tools we use change. The effective date at the top shows when it was last updated.
